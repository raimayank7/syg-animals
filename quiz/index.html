Animal quiz

Ten questions each round. Pick the right answer from four.

Quiz type:

Play it together

Read the question aloud and let everyone guess before you tap. After each answer, a link opens the animal's page, so a wrong answer becomes something new learnt. Study the lists first if you like: baby names and group names.

The three kinds of question

  • Baby names: you are shown an animal and asked what its young one is called. Cub, calf, kit, pup and chick cover many animals, so watch for the odd ones, such as joey, cygnet and squab.
  • Group names: you are asked for the collective noun, such as a pride of lions. Some animals have two accepted names. The quiz counts the one given on that animal's page.
  • Which kind of animal?: you sort the animal into mammal, bird, reptile, amphibian, fish or invertebrate. The catches are the animals that live in an unexpected place: the bat, the whale, the penguin and the sea turtle. The page on groups of animals explains how to decide.

How scoring works

Each round has ten questions drawn at random from the animals on this site, so no two rounds are the same. You get one try at each question, and the right answer is shown straight away. Your best score for each kind of quiz is kept in this browser only. Nothing is sent anywhere, and clearing your browser data clears the scores. For a class or a party, split into two teams and take turns: the team with more right answers out of ten wins the round.
