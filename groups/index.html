Groups of animals

Animals are sorted into groups by the features they share. These six are the ones taught first in school.

  • Mammals 66 animals
  • Birds 20 animals
  • Reptiles 12 animals
  • Amphibians 4 animals
  • Fish 8 animals
  • Invertebrates 12 animals

Vertebrates and invertebrates

The first big split is the backbone. Animals with a backbone are vertebrates: mammals, birds, reptiles, amphibians and fish. Animals without one are invertebrates. Vertebrates are the animals we notice most, but invertebrates are far greater in number and variety.

Warm-blooded and cold-blooded

Mammals and birds are warm-blooded: their bodies make heat and keep a steady temperature, so they can stay active in the cold, but they need a lot of food. Reptiles, amphibians, fish and invertebrates are cold-blooded: their temperature follows their surroundings, so they need far less food but slow down when it is cold.
