Barn owl

Tyto alba · Bird · Carnivore

The barn owl has a pale, heart-shaped face that works like a dish to collect sound. It can catch mice in total darkness by hearing alone, and its soft feathers let it fly almost silently.

How it is classified

Scientists sort living things into groups within groups. Reading down, each step is a smaller group, ending with the species itself.

  1. Kingdom Animalia
  2. Phylum Chordata
  3. Class Aves
  4. Order Strigiformes
  5. Family Tytonidae
  6. Genus Tyto
  7. Species Tyto alba

Classification follows the GBIF Backbone Taxonomy. Other reference works arrange some groups differently. How classification works.

Is it in danger?

The barn owl is listed as Least Concern. The species is widespread or numerous enough that it is not considered at risk of extinction at present.

This is the IUCN Red List category as recorded in Wikidata on 11 November 2026. The IUCN Red List itself is the authority and may carry a newer assessment. What the categories mean.
