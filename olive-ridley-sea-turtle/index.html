Olive ridley sea turtle

Lepidochelys olivacea · Reptile · Omnivore

The olive ridley is a small sea turtle known for mass nesting, when many thousands of females come ashore together over a few nights. This event is called an arribada. The beaches of Odisha, such as Gahirmatha and Rushikulya, are among the most important in the world.

Is it in danger?

The olive ridley sea turtle is listed as Vulnerable. The species faces a high risk of extinction in the wild.

This is the IUCN Red List category as recorded in Wikidata on 11 November 2026. The IUCN Red List itself is the authority and may carry a newer assessment. What the categories mean.
