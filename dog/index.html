Dog

Canis lupus familiaris · Mammal · Omnivore

The dog was the first animal to be domesticated. Dogs have a far keener sense of smell than people, which is why they are trained to find missing persons and to detect explosives. There are hundreds of breeds, from tiny lap dogs to large working dogs.

What the dog eats

Dogs came from meat-eating wolves, but thousands of years beside people have changed them. They digest starchy food such as rice and roti far better than wolves do. A healthy diet for a dog still needs plenty of protein. Some human foods harm dogs, among them chocolate, grapes and onions.

How it lives

Dogs are social animals and treat the family as their pack. They read human faces, voices and pointing fingers remarkably well. Puppies are born blind and deaf and open their eyes at around two weeks. The first three months of life shape how a dog will behave with people and other animals later.

How to tell it apart

Breeds differ more in size and shape than any other mammal, from the tiny Chihuahua to the Great Dane. India's own village and street dog, often called the Indian pariah dog or desi dog, is a hardy, medium-sized animal with upright ears and a curled tail. It is one of the oldest types of dog in the world.

The dog and people

Dogs guard homes and herds, guide blind people, find people after earthquakes and work with the police and army. A dog bite always needs washing with soap and water and a visit to a doctor, because dogs can carry rabies. Vaccinating pet and street dogs protects both the dogs and the people around them.

How it is classified

Scientists sort living things into groups within groups. Reading down, each step is a smaller group, ending with the species itself.

  1. Kingdom Animalia
  2. Phylum Chordata
  3. Class Mammalia
  4. Order Carnivora
  5. Family Canidae
  6. Genus Canis
  7. Species Canis lupus familiaris

Other members of the family Canidae on this site: red fox, wolf.

More distant relatives in the order Carnivora: brown bear, cheetah, domestic cat, giant panda, jaguar, leopard, lion, polar bear, red panda, sloth bear, snow leopard, spotted hyena.

Classification follows the GBIF Backbone Taxonomy. Other reference works arrange some groups differently. How classification works.

Is it in danger?

The dog is a domestic animal, kept and bred by people. Domestic animals are not assessed for the Red List of threatened species.
